Images for facilities go here. Reference them as /images/facilities/<file>.

The first image in a facility's `images` array is its card photo. Cards are
**4:5 portrait** and crop with `object-cover`, and the lower third sits under a
dark scrim carrying the title — so keep the subject in the upper two thirds.

1000 × 1250 or larger. The files here now are flat placeholders; overwrite them
in place, the paths are set in `src/content/facilities.ts`.
