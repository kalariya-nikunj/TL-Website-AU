Images for workshops and events go here. Reference them as /images/events/<file>.

An event's `image` is its card photo. Cards are **4:5 portrait** and crop with
`object-cover`, and the lower third sits under a dark scrim carrying the title —
so keep the subject in the upper two thirds.

1000 × 1250 or larger. `image` is optional: an event without one falls back to
the workshops page hero. The files here now are flat placeholders; overwrite
them in place, the paths are set in `src/content/events.ts`.
